# Changelog

All notable changes to Kvikkshot are listed here. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-21

The first release, on the Mac App Store and as a download from tang-spark.no.

- Screenshots from a video by interval or by count, in a fast keyframe mode or exact to the frame, as full-resolution JPG or PNG.
- Several videos at once, processed one after another, and exported together with a folder for each.
- Share images: the selected screenshots on one sheet, with adjustable columns, width, dark or light background, timestamps and video details.
- Settings for a default export folder, JPEG quality, and the "Made with Kvikkshot" line on share images.
- English, English (UK), Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.
- Runs in the App Sandbox with no network access. Cached screenshots are deleted on Back, on quit, and at the next launch after a crash.
