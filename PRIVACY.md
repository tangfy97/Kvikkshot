# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 21 October 2026*

Kvikkshot is a macOS app for taking screenshots from videos, made by TangSpark. This policy covers every version of Kvikkshot: the Mac App Store version and the one downloaded from GitHub.

**Kvikkshot collects no data.** It has no network access at all; the operating system's App Sandbox does not allow it to connect to the internet. There are no accounts, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising, or update checks, and no third-party code.

What Kvikkshot does on your Mac:

- It reads the videos you open or drop onto it, to make screenshots from them.
- It keeps those screenshots in its own temporary folder inside the app's sandbox while you work. They are deleted when you go back to the start screen, when you quit, and, if Kvikkshot was quit unexpectedly, the next time it starts.
- It writes files only where you ask: the folder you choose when you export, or the place you save a share image.
- It remembers your settings, the last folder you exported to, and, if you choose one in Settings, your default export folder. These stay on your Mac.

None of this leaves your Mac. Links in the Help menu open in your web browser, which is outside Kvikkshot; "Check for a New Version" in the downloaded version tells the Kvikkshot web page which version you have, so it can say whether a newer one exists.
